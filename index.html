Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 November 2026

This policy explains how Dr. Apsara’s Smile Dental Clinic (“we”, “us”) handles your personal information when you use this website. We are a dental clinic in Hanamkonda, Telangana, India.

1. What we collect

  • Appointment details you enter in the booking form: your name, phone number, chosen treatment, and preferred date and time.
  • Chat messages you type into the website assistant.
  • WhatsApp messages: if you tap “Book via WhatsApp”, your details are sent through WhatsApp, which has its own privacy policy.
  • Technical data: this site loads fonts from Google Fonts, so your IP address is shared with Google when the page loads.

Please do not enter detailed medical history, reports or other sensitive health information in the form or the chat. We will collect that in person, with your consent.

2. How we use it

We use your details to contact you, confirm or reschedule appointments, answer your questions and run the clinic. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

3. AI assistant

When AI replies are switched on, your chat messages are sent through our secure server to an AI service provider so it can write a reply. Replies are automated and are not medical advice. Avoid sharing personal details in the chat.

4. Who can see it

Our clinic staff, and the service providers that host this website and its booking records or generate chat replies, only as needed to run these services. We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

We keep appointment requests for as long as needed to manage your care and our records, then delete them. You can ask us to delete them sooner.

6. Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information. No method of sending data over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your rights

Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may ask to know what data we hold about you, correct it, erase it, or withdraw your consent, and you may raise a grievance with us. Contact us using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

8. Children

If the patient is under 18, a parent or legal guardian should make the booking and give consent on their behalf.
